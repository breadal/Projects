About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Computing at Dublin City University (DCU).

My research focuses on connected health, healthcare information systems, artificial intelligence, multimodal data analytics and intelligent healthcare systems.

Research Areas

  • Applied Analytics
  • Multimodal AI
  • Machine Learning
  • Connected Health
  • Medical Informatics
  • Digital Health
  • Healthcare Data Integration

Current Research Projects

Interactive Oral Assessment

Developing an intelligent oral assessment platform that automatically conducts and manages oral examinations through AI-powered interaction. The system can ask questions, analyse student responses, and provide automated assessment and feedback.

AI-Generated Immersive Career Environments

Developing an AI-driven platform that automatically generates immersive 3D environments representing different careers and workplaces. The project explores how AI and virtual environments can support career exploration and education.

Connected Health: Healthcare Integration and Architecture

Developing an intelligent Connected Health architecture that integrates heterogeneous healthcare data from hospitals, wearable devices, and other health information sources. The project focuses on semantic interoperability, data integration, and AI-enabled healthcare services.

Multimodal AI for Healthcare

Developing AI methods that combine clinical, imaging, speech, and other healthcare modalities to support intelligent diagnosis, prediction, and clinical decision-making.

Entity Resolution

Methods for identifying and linking records representing the same entity across multiple data sources.
